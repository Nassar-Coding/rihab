# Real Madinah places

Rihab Madinah lists 197 real places in Madinah (the content workbook's real places, with its ids). Each sits in the
district its sources give; places the sources locate only as "in Madinah" show the city as their area and put no pin on
the map. Map positions are schematic (a district's approximate direction and distance from the Prophet's Mosque), never GPS.

## What the app says about a real place — and what it doesn't

- Its name, its kind, its district and a short description from the sources.
- No opening hours, prices, ratings or offers: none of that was verified, so none is shown, and no illustrative offer,
  event or organiser is ever attached to a real place or business.
- No sources, confidence or review dates on screen: that provenance stays in the data and in this file.
